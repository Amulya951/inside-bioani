# Photos

Drop real photographs here. Nothing else to configure: the newsletter finds each
person's photo by their name, exactly as written in `data/employees.js`.

**Naming rule:** full name, spaces replaced with underscores, `.jpg`.
"Gurpal Singh" → `Gurpal_Singh.jpg`. Capital letters must match.

If a photo is missing or fails to load, the page shows a monogram placeholder,
never a broken image. You can also set a different filename per person with the
`photo` field in `data/employees.js`.

**Size:** crop portraits to 4:5, about 800 × 1000 px, and compress to roughly
100–250 KB each. The single-file build embeds every photo in this folder, and the
published page must stay under 16 MB in total (build.py warns at 15.5 MB).

Only use real photos supplied by the person or HR. No stock, AI or look-alike images.
